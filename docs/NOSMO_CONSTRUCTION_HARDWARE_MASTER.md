## 3. Product direction - NOSMO EXO-01

The first generation should be practical, mechanically credible and narrow in scope.

### Core concept

- Passive or predominantly mechanical full-body load-bearing frame.
- Load transfer through back/spine structure, hips and legs.
- Articulated tool-support arm connected to the exoskeleton frame.
- Heavy tool weight supported by the frame rather than by the operator alone.
- Onboard power supply carried by the exoskeleton itself.
- No trailing power cable as the primary concept.
- Power wiring routed through the frame/tool-support arm.
- Modular / quick-change tool interface.
- Generic multi-brand tool compatibility where technically feasible.
- Worker remains mobile and directly controls the tool.

### First target use cases

Priority 1:
- heavy concrete drilling;
- anchor / fixing installation;
- overhead drilling and installation.

Priority 2:
- demolition / chipping / breaker support;
- MEP overhead installation;
- structural steel bolting / heavy tool handling;
- formwork and repetitive heavy fixing.

Later evaluation:
- road works;
- rebar work;
- tunnelling / infrastructure;
- other heavy construction tasks.

Bricklaying is not the preferred first use case because it demands continuous walking, twisting, bending and free body movement.

### Gen-1 design principle

Do not attempt to build an "Iron Man" suit.

The first prototype should prove one measurable construction benefit with the minimum viable hardware architecture.

Candidate validation test:
- repetitive heavy drilling with and without EXO-01;
- compare operator fatigue, task time, stability, accuracy and perceived load.

The preferred structure should protect both NOSMO and the technical founder without imposing unnecessary global restrictions.

### Background IP

Andrea / the existing Morph owner should retain ownership of pre-existing Morph IP unless a later signed agreement explicitly states otherwise.

NOSMO should not require ownership of unrelated historical Morph technology as a condition of collaboration.

### Foreground / construction-specific IP

Technology created specifically through the joint construction project should be clearly allocated in a signed agreement.

Preferred principle:
- construction-specific foreground IP is owned or controlled by the new joint company;
- this may include construction tool-support systems, mounts, load-transfer adaptations, onboard power integration, construction safety architecture, construction-specific interfaces and other jointly funded developments.

### Exclusivity

Do not impose a blanket worldwide non-compete on Andrea.

Preferred commercial protection:
- exclusive construction / civil-engineering commercial rights for the joint venture in the UK and Poland;
- possible right of first negotiation or right of first refusal for additional European construction markets;
- Andrea remains free to work on Morph in other sectors, subject to protection of joint-company foreground IP and confidential information.

Potential construction field should include:
- building construction;
- civil engineering;
- infrastructure;
- road works;
- related heavy construction use cases.

### Non-circumvention

Customers, test partners, distributors, investors and commercial contacts introduced through NOSMO should not be bypassed by either party for the same construction product opportunity.

### Reserved matters

Even if one founder holds a simple majority of shares, major decisions should require enhanced approval.

Candidate reserved matters:
- sale or exclusive licence of core construction IP;
- sale of the company;
- change of control;
- issue of new shares;
- material dilution;
- licensing construction technology to a direct competitor;
- disposal of major IP;
- removal of agreed NOSMO branding / field rights;
- material change to the construction business scope.

### Equity

No percentage is approved yet.

Working idea only:

## 6. Funding - current verified position

### DeepTech Goes Dual - current status

As of 2026-09-19, PARP has closed the operator-selection call for **Startup Booster Poland - DeepTech Goes Dual**.

The operator call ran from 2026-07-14 to 2026-09-08.

The current PARP process is selecting accelerator operators. Startup applications are not yet open directly through this call.

PARP states that the programme is intended for deep-tech startups, including technologies with potential civil and military / dual-use applications.

### Startup support limits

Official PARP documentation states that support for one startup under the Acceleration activity may not exceed:

**Stage I**
- PLN 450,000 grant;
- PLN 50,000 free accelerator services.

**Stage II**
- PLN 450,000 grant;
- PLN 50,000 free accelerator services.

Total grant value for one startup cannot exceed the PLN equivalent of EUR 200,000.

VAT is not an eligible project cost under the operator rules.

### TRL requirement

PARP states:
- minimum entry level: TRL 4;
- Stage I must advance the technology by at least two TRL levels;
- startups above TRL 4 may participate;
- Stage I should therefore normally accept technology no higher than TRL 7;
- Stage I may last up to 12 months;
- the full Acceleration activity may last up to 16 months.

### Programme timing

The official operator rules require the first acceleration round to be planned to start no later than **2027-02-01**.

"Start of a round" relates to the accelerator programme, including startup recruitment.

The exact startup call dates and individual operator rules are not yet known.

### IMPORTANT - 100% funding wording

Do **not** externally claim yet that the individual NOSMO / Morph startup project is guaranteed 100% funding with zero own contribution.

What is currently verified:
- PARP states that the accelerator operator project can be funded up to 100% of eligible costs;
- startup grants are explicitly provided as cash grants within the accelerator programme, subject to caps and de minimis rules.

What still needs confirmation:

- treatment of equipment, materials, founder salaries, subcontracting, certification and prototype manufacturing.

Those details must be checked in the startup recruitment rules published by the selected operators.
