# UNICON TOOL — HINGE LOAD & DOOR BALANCE (CANON)

## PURPOSE
Detects improper load distribution on door hinges and identifies door leaf twist,
sag, or self-movement caused by mechanical imbalance.

This tool focuses on REAL load behavior, not visual alignment only.

---

## CORE PROBLEMS DETECTED
A door may appear plumb but still:
- open or close by itself
- create uneven gaps
- overload top or bottom hinge
- twist under its own weight

---

## MEASUREMENT MODES

### 1. STATIC HINGE LOAD
Measures load bias on:
- TOP hinge
- MIDDLE hinge (if present)
- BOTTOM hinge

Indicates whether load is correctly transferred through the hinge line.

---

### 2. DOOR LEAF TWIST
Detects torsion of the door leaf by comparing:
- top vs bottom plane
- hinge side vs lock side

---

### 3. SELF-MOVEMENT TEST
Detects if the door:
- opens forward
- opens backward
- closes by itself

Movement is quantified, not guessed.

---

## VISUAL FEEDBACK (HUD)

Color coding:
- GREEN — correct load / neutral behavior
- YELLOW — minor imbalance (acceptable, but monitor)
- RED — incorrect load or twist (adjustment required)

Arrows show direction of force transfer.

---

## NOTES
This tool is used AFTER:
- frame is installed
- hinges are fixed
- door leaf is hung

This tool is NOT used for:
- frame plumbing
- gap sizing
- client acceptance

---

## COMPLETION
Tool passes when:
- hinge loads are balanced
- no self-movement detected
- no torsional twist above tolerance
