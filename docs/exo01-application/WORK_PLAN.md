## 3.1b Task descriptions

### WP1 — requirements and task definition

Objective: make construction use cases measurable and testable without prematurely broadening the demonstrator.

T1.1 N leads structured user/task discovery with contractor access to be evidenced. R identifies ergonomic questions; M checks engineering feasibility. T1.2 N/M/R specify primary drilling/fixing task and tool envelope, PPE, anthropometry, working positions, release and mobility requirements. T1.3 R defines outcome hierarchy and feasibility constraints; N records industrial/manufacturing relevance and stakeholder evidence. Output D1.1 includes traceability from task to design requirement and measurement. Recruitment, site access and interview consent remain subject to agreement.

### WP2 — passive full-body architecture

Objective: investigate load-transfer geometry, joint alignment and adjustment while preserving user control.

T2.1 M provides a verified background/foreground boundary and evidence baseline. T2.2 M develops competing passive load paths with R modelling human reactions, contact pressure and motion constraints. T2.3 M builds component rigs; R compares predictions and observations; N reviews PPE/task compatibility. D2.1 records alternatives, model uncertainty and selection rationale, not just a chosen CAD assembly. Any unacceptable reaction-load or instability result returns the design to review.

### WP3 — tool, load-transfer and power architecture

Objective: investigate a modular articulated support and isolation/routing architecture for the selected task.

T3.1 M/N specify tool coupling, force/moment envelope, retention and rapid release. T3.2 M/R compare passive balancing and vibration-isolation concepts with instrumented bench tests. T3.3 M investigates power-source positioning and safe routing without claiming compatibility with proprietary tool systems. T3.4 N/R assess reach, operator control and module-change practicality. D3.1 documents interfaces, bench data and design limits. Tool-maker permissions and electrical expertise must be secured where needed.

### WP4 — biomechanics, vibration and safety

Objective: provide independent models and an ethically approved evaluation method.

T4.1 R develops coupled modelling and selects sensors, calibration and reproducibility checks. T4.2 R/M perform hazard analysis, failure/load-transfer review and non-human bench testing. T4.3 R prepares ethics, consent, data governance and pre-specified statistical protocol; primary endpoint, sample size and numeric safety limits remain TBD. T4.4 R validates measurement reliability and issues the human-study release recommendation only after institutional requirements are satisfied. N contributes work-practice context. D4.1 precedes recruitment; D4.2 records bench/measurement evidence. Tests are not a substitute for certification.

### WP5 — integrated laboratory demonstrator

Objective: integrate only the selected research components and document laboratory feasibility.

T5.1 M assembles the frame, arm, interface, power carriage and isolation components. T5.2 M/R verify structural/interface behaviour and instrumentation; N reviews task fidelity. T5.3 M records configuration, build provenance, maintenance and repeatable setup. D5.1 includes demonstrator configuration and test record. No operational construction-site deployment is included in the proposed TRL4 endpoint.

### WP6 — controlled validation and relevance

Objective: compare baseline and assistance under the approved laboratory protocol and report benefits and tradeoffs.

T6.1 R recruits approved participants and runs familiarisation/calibration after all release gates. T6.2 R performs counterbalanced baseline/assisted comparisons and records EMG, movement, forces, tool accuracy, comfort and vibration; N ensures task relevance; U supplies feedback only under an agreed arrangement. T6.3 R analyses effect sizes and uncertainty, including adverse/null findings; N assesses transfer limits to real workplaces. D6.1 provides a reproducible report and access-controlled data package. D6.2 is a construction/industrial relevance and follow-on validation specification, not proof of field readiness.

### WP7 — IP, exploitation and commercial pathway

Objective: govern research results and prepare an evidence-based route beyond the project.

T7.1 All partners identify background, ownership and needed access; negotiate consortium arrangements and result protection without inventing JV shares. T7.2 N coordinates customer/use-path evidence and compares sale, leasing, hire/fleet and tool-partner channels already described in the October master. T7.3 All partners disseminate reviewed findings and maintain a result/market/qualification roadmap. T7.4 Proposed coordinator manages reporting, quality, risk/change control and decision logs; R leads data/ethics coordination and all partners review the data-management plan. Coordination effort must be budgeted explicitly. A JV remains an option, not a grant commitment.

## 3.1e Critical risks

| Risk | Likelihood / severity (proposed) | Mitigation / trigger / candidate owner |
|---|---|---|
| Existing technology too mature or insufficient novelty | Medium / High | Evidence-led component boundary and prior-art matrix before proposal freeze; M/R |
| Construction case lacks SRIA manufacturing relevance | Medium / High | Validate industrial task and contribution with stakeholder/NCP; N |
| Load reduced at shoulder increases strain elsewhere | Medium / High | Multi-region force/motion/EMG assessment; redesign/stop rather than claim success; R/M |
| Isolation amplifies a relevant frequency or destabilises tool | Medium / High | Instrumented bench comparison before people; reject unsafe configurations; M/R |
| Fit, motion or PPE incompatibility | Medium / High | Early anthropometric/kinematic review and explicit fit envelope; M/N/R |
| Background access or ownership unresolved | Medium / High | Written schedule of ownership/access before reliance or integration; all |
| Ethics delay or participant recruitment failure | Medium / High | Early institutional review; feasible protocol; no unapproved substitute recruitment; R |
| Partner lacks engineering/research capacity | Medium / High | Named teams, equipment availability, bottom-up effort and alternates before final commitment; coordinator TBD |
| Resource/cost estimate cannot support seven WPs | Medium / High | Cost the task plan and narrow scope before final budget; all |
