## Verified call conditions — concise rule digest

The topic is a single-stage RIA under call HORIZON-CL4-2027-06 (INDUSTRY-FTRI), despite the topic identifier retaining `2027-01`. The work programme sets up to EUR 2.5m per project, EUR 20m topic funding, breakthrough research up to TRL 4, explicit SRIA alignment and at most six independent eligible participants. It gives evaluation feedback within four months and grant signature within six months of deadline. Results transfers/exclusive licences may face authority objection for four years after project end. [S1, pp.21–22,74–75]

General rules require three independent beneficiaries in distinct Member/Associated countries, including one Member State; PIC registration, civil-only focus, relevant GEP eligibility and ethics compliance apply. Full RIA scoring: Excellence, Impact, Implementation, each /5; thresholds 3 each and 10 total. RIA funding is 100% of eligible costs. Default full-proposal limit is 40 pages (45 for lump-sum RIA), subject to system-specific instructions. Forms supplied inside submission system govern. [S2, Annexes A–G]

UK association permits participation on similar terms to Member States from 2024; this does not establish NOSMO's individual legal eligibility. [S5]
