## C. UK Cluster 4 Industry NCP — fit / eligibility check

**To:** NCP-Industry@iuk.ukri.org

**Subject:** HORIZON-CL4-2027-01-MAT-PROD-61 — topic-fit check for passive industrial exoskeleton research

Dear Cluster 4 Industry NCP,

I would appreciate a brief eligibility / topic-fit check for a consortium concept we are developing for HORIZON-CL4-2027-01-MAT-PROD-61.

The consortium is currently being formed around:
- a UK construction-technology SME (NOSMO Technology);
- an Italian exoskeleton engineering startup with existing passive full-body background technology;
- a Polish biomechanics / biomechatronics research partner now being approached.

The proposed research object is not simply commercialisation of the existing exoskeleton. It is a new construction / industrial heavy-tool augmentation architecture at early TRL, covering passive full-body load transfer, articulated heavy-tool support, vibration isolation, frame-mounted tool power and construction-specific human-factors validation.

We are deliberately separating the higher-TRL background platform from the new research components so that the proposal does not artificially downgrade existing technology.

Could you please advise whether this appears to be a credible thematic / TRL fit for MAT-PROD-61 under the Made in Europe SRIA, and whether there are any obvious eligibility or scope concerns we should address before we invest further in consortium building?

I would also be interested in confirming whether NOSMO Technology may be eligible for the UK Horizon Europe Springboard Scheme for Cluster 4 Industry proposal-development support.
