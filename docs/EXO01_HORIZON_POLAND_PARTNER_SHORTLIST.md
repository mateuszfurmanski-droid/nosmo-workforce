# EXO-01 Horizon Partner Shortlist — Poland

**Status:** INTERNAL / OUTREACH NOT YET SENT  
**Date:** 2026-09-26

## 1. Gdansk University of Technology — Laboratory of Biomechanics

**Why it fits**
- non-invasive human biomechanical testing;
- surface EMG;
- IMU;
- force plates;
- 14-camera optical motion capture;
- human-robot interaction;
- testing of mechatronic / robotic devices;
- explicit cooperation offer for testing people using external assistive devices and prototypes.

**Potential EXO-01 role**
- human factors;
- load-transfer validation;
- EMG comparison;
- operator movement;
- ergonomics;
- human-machine interaction;
- validation protocol.

**Public contact**
- Head: Wiktoria Wojnicz, Associate Professor
- Section / laboratory public page:
  https://wimio.pg.edu.pl/en/faculty-labs/laboratory-biomechanics
- Public faculty contact shown online:
  wiktoria.wojnicz@pg.edu.pl

**Assessment**
Strongest immediate Polish partner for independent biomechanics and operator validation.

## 2. AGH University of Krakow — Biomechatronic Systems Research Team

**Why it fits**
- research on biomechanical and anthropotechnical systems;
- assistive movement devices;
- active exoskeletons;
- advanced vision, inertial and strain measurement;
- real-world and laboratory motion studies;
- analytical and numerical modelling;
- finite element methods;
- structural optimisation of biomechatronic systems.

Recent public work also includes optimisation and human testing of a lightweight exoskeleton.

**Potential EXO-01 role**
- biomechanical modelling;
- structural optimisation;
- load paths;
- simulation;
- exoskeleton mechanics;
- design optimisation;
- test methodology.

**Public pages**
- Research team:
  https://oferta-badawcza.agh.edu.pl/research-teams/zespol-badania-i-projektowania-ukladow-biomechatronicznych-00397/
- Related 2026 exoskeleton publication:
  https://badap.agh.edu.pl/publikacja/166738

**Assessment**
Very strong technical / modelling partner, particularly if the proposal needs deeper research content to defend the <=TRL4 Horizon framing.
