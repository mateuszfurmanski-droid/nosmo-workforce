# ADDON: NOSMO Work Light, Training and Skills Tree

Status: product backlog only. Not implemented in V1.0095.

## Product decision

Launch with the full NOSMO Work app. A future Light edition can become a simpler entry point for workers whose immediate needs are agency communication, limited work and tools search, and short training sessions. Light must not remove or downgrade the current full product.

## Light edition boundary

Light should include:

- Worker identity and live availability;
- agency contacts and one-agency messaging;
- a safe agency-request reply pack;
- limited Ask Nexus searches for work and tools/materials;
- Training and Skills Tree;
- a clear upgrade path to the full product.

Light should exclude or limit advanced document registers, drawings, project evidence, larger search batches, automation and professional workflow tools. Exact commercial limits must be decided later and must be visible before a worker reaches them.

## Training and new certificates

The learning unit should be designed for five-to-ten-minute sessions:

1. Nexus reads the worker's approved cards, certificates, skills and job goals.
2. It suggests a practical next qualification and explains what it may unlock.
3. The worker learns through small lessons and short quizzes.
4. Progress is saved after every question.
5. Completion unlocks an exam or assessment handoff.
6. Any certificate purchase shows the awarding body, price, prerequisites, renewal rules and refund terms before payment.

NOSMO must never imply that a quiz is an accredited qualification. A certificate can be offered only through a verified accredited provider or awarding body, and the provider remains the issuer.

## Skills Tree

Each node should store:

- skill or qualification name;
- evidence already held;
- prerequisites;
- recommended preparation;
- assessment provider;
- expected time and cost;
- expiry or renewal rule;
- roles and pay bands it may help unlock;
- confidence and the source date for every recommendation.

The tree should show three states: achieved, available next and locked. A worker should always see why a node is recommended and what concrete role or responsibility it could enable.

Example pathways to validate against current UK awarding bodies and employers before launch:

- Joinery -> fire-door awareness -> installation competence -> inspection/remedial specialism;
- Skilled Worker CSCS -> supervisor training -> site-management pathway;
- Site operative -> PASMA/IPAF or task-specific plant access;
- General construction -> first aid, asbestos awareness and role-specific safety modules.

These are product examples, not claims of legal eligibility. Current prerequisites and recognition must be verified at implementation time.

## Future delivery sequence

1. Training catalogue and verified provider records.
2. Worker evidence-to-skill matching.
3. Quiz engine and resumable progress.
4. Skills Tree recommendations.
5. Exam booking and transparent purchase handoff.
6. Light-edition packaging and limits.
